Connect Spotify

Tide plays your music in this window, pauses it when a break starts and picks it back up when you focus. Spotify asks you to register a small developer app first. It takes about two minutes and needs Spotify Premium.

  1. Open the Spotify developer dashboard and log in with your Spotify account.

    Open the dashboard

  2. Choose Create app. The name and description can be anything. Add this as the redirect URI:

    Under Which API/SDKs are you planning to use? tick Web API and Web Playback SDK, agree to the terms and save.

  3. Open your app’s Settings, copy the Client ID under Basic Information and paste it here.

If Spotify later says your account isn’t registered for the app, open the app’s settings in the dashboard, go to User Management and add the email address of your Spotify account.
